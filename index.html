Our Story

More Than a Cut.
A Ritual.

Since 1994, Iron & Edge has been the cornerstone of gentlemen's grooming in the city. What began as a single chair and a passion for the craft has grown into a full-service establishment trusted by thousands.

Our barbers are artisans — trained in traditional techniques, fluent in modern styles. Every visit is a ceremony: the warm towel, the straight razor, the precise line. You'll feel it from the moment you sit down.
